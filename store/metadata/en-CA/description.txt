One camera roll for the whole group.

With CamTrip everyone in your group shoots photos with a retro camera, and everyone sees them right away. No more endless "can you send me the photos?" messages. After the vacation, the party or the weekend away you share one album, plus a recap video that makes itself.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a group: trip (with start and end date), event (one day or evening) or always open (your regular friend group)
• Invite friends with a 6-character code, a QR code or a link
• Shoot photos and short videos with the retro camera
• Every photo is shared with the group instantly

RETRO CAMERA STYLES
• Film - warm colours, grain and an orange date stamp
• Instant - soft colours and a white border
• Disposable - harsh flash, vignette and light leaks
• B&W, Golden and Chrome - extra styles with CamTrip Pro

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Gallery with a timeline per day
• Reactions and favourites
• Filter by person: see every photo from one friend
• Download other people's photos with one tap
• Every photo you take is also saved to your own album "CamTrip - [group name]"

AUTOMATIC RECAP VIDEO
At the end, CamTrip makes a recap video of your best moments. Pick a template and royalty-free music, and share it right away.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
No internet at the campsite or in the mountains? Keep shooting. Your photos are already in your album and get shared as soon as you are back online.

CALM NOTIFICATIONS
Notifications about new photos are bundled, so your phone does not buzz all day.

FREE
• Up to 10 members per group
• 3 camera styles: Film, Instant and Disposable
• Free groups are removed 30 days after the end date, so save your favourites in time

CAMTRIP PRO
• All camera styles (B&W, Golden, Chrome)
• Unlimited members and storage, photos are kept forever
• Recap videos without watermark
• Download in original quality
• One Pro member unlocks Pro for the whole group
